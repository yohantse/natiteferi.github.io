// About

Building Africa's
Digital Economy
from Addis Ababa

Nati Teferi is the Founder and CEO of Arif-Tech Group and its flagship platform ArifGet — a leading expert in the online work and freelancing space, building a strong reputation for providing high-quality training and educational opportunities to aspiring and experienced remote professionals.

His programs focus on two key pillars: leveraging AI-powered tools to enhance productivity, and mastering digital marketing strategies to build personal brands and reach global audiences.

From hands-on workshops at Addis Ababa University to collaborative events with ALX Ethiopia, AWAQI, and SAND Technologies — and media features on BBC, EBS TV, and Abay TV — Nati is reshaping how Ethiopian youth engage with the global digital economy.

Tech Founder AI Tools Freelancing Digital Marketing Remote Work Youth Empowerment EdTech

Vision

To become the leading platform in Africa that empowers individuals with practical digital skills, AI tools, and freelancing opportunities — enabling them to work, earn, and grow in the global digital economy.

Mission

To provide accessible, high-quality training in freelancing, AI tools, and digital marketing through courses, workshops, and real-world experiences — equipping individuals with the skills, knowledge, and confidence to build sustainable careers.
